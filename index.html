About Buddy Connect

Many international students in India tell us the same things: they miss home, find it hard to talk with classmates, and don't know where to start with the food, customs and city.

Buddy Connect matches each new student with an Indian buddy who shares a language or an interest. Buddies show you around campus, explain local ways, and become the first friend you can message when something feels unfamiliar.

We designed this prototype for a Design Thinking project, from interviews with students who have lived this experience.

🤝
